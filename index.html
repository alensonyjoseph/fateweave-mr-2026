THE LIVING BOARD

Command a living miniature army through real chess. During AI duels, you can spend one of three Fate Weaves to make a projected two-move future real. Every move remains legally validated; other modes retain standard chess.

  1. Choose a warrior. Click it on desktop, or point and pinch on Meta Quest. Glowing squares show legal destinations.
  2. Choose a square. Click or pinch a highlighted destination; the warrior will move or attack.
  3. Face the AI. Ivory moves first; Crimson responds automatically. Switch AI / Two Players to play both sides on the same board.
  4. Weave a future. In an AI duel, select an ivory warrior and press FATE WEAVE. Up to three translucent timelines appear, including a predicted rival reply. Pinch one ghost to make both legal moves happen. You have three Fate charges per game. In local or online two-player mode, futures remain previews only.
  5. Play online. Open Online Friend before entering XR. The host sends an invitation code, the guest sends a reply, then both players see the same legal moves. Network restrictions may prevent direct peer-to-peer connections.
  6. Command by voice or text. Try “e2 to e4”, “knight to f3”, “Fate Sight” or “undo”. Microphone recognition is browser-dependent; pinching always works.
  7. Play again or undo. Rewind moves during local practice. Online matches disable undo to prevent desynchronization. On Quest, the six front-edge sigils offer spatial controls.

Quest scene understanding may place the board on a recognised table in your room. Animated cinematic duels, enhanced assets and headset-specific performance tuning remain in development.
